1 · Control screenThe operator's tablet, behind a PIN. One button per step, live numbers, and alerts if anything drops. A second tablet on its own hotspot is the backup.
2 · Big screensA laptop feeding the LED walls. It follows the server, and if the connection drops it plays the show by itself, so the crowd never sees an error.
3 · Crowd phonesOpened from one QR code, no app to install. Rally photo first, then a Photo / Vote toggle. Every phone jumps to the next step when the operator taps.
Rally photo frameA selfie in an NDP frame: the logo, "Anniversary Rally" and "Geh Dem Performance". The front camera opens with the frame on and a 3-2-1 countdown. The photo is made on the phone, never uploaded, and shared straight to WhatsApp, Facebook or Instagram. The Photo tab stays open all night.
Share to social mediaThe Share button opens the phone's own share menu, so it works with WhatsApp, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, X, Status or any app installed. The caption is filled in with #GEHDEMPERFORMANCE. Every share is free reach for the party.
Send to the big screenA third button sends the photo to the big screens; people abroad use their own diaspora link. Photos wait for approval, 20 at a time, with a waiting line that sends automatically. The wall holds 15 venue and 5 diaspora photos. Nothing goes up unapproved, and all photos are deleted after the rally.
Live fireworks (optional)The victory moment is guaranteed by the timer, so it can be matched with real fireworks. The control screen shows the fireworks crew a countdown cue. The licensed crew fires on that cue; the system never sets off fireworks by itself.
QR code, any timeThe operator can put the QR code back on the big screens whenever they like: before the doors open, between speeches, after the replay. Latecomers get in all night.
Live serverSends the operator's taps out to every screen and adds up votes and shakes. Phones send small batched tallies, so thousands can join at once.
