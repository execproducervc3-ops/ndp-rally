EVERYTHING IN EVENT PAL FOR SPORT

The full list, for a national tournament.

Tournament

  • Teams, venues and dates entered once; AVA builds the fixture list
  • Change a date and every screen, ticket and report follows
  • The same set-up at every ground, on every island
  • Results go straight into the points table and player statistics
  • A record that builds season after season

Tickets & gates

  • Day tickets, tournament passes and family days
  • Sold online and at outlets; a printed ticket only works once it's sold
  • Free school and youth passes, counted at the gate
  • Any phone is a gate scanner; secured codes stop fakes and screenshots
  • Live crowd per venue, capacity alerts from AVA, the same numbers for police and stewards

Screens & scoring

  • Scoreboards broadcast in 4K for maximum clarity at the venue
  • The scorer's pad: every ball, goal or point updates the board instantly
  • Cricket (T20, one-day, Test), football, netball and athletics
  • Team colours and crests on the scoreboard, line-ups and statistics
  • One national standard: the same look at every venue and in every sport
  • Replays, announcements and sponsor slots on the same screens
  • Streaming to the diaspora and other islands, free or with a paid pass

Grounds & money

  • Every food stall and bar on one till system, by stall and by match
  • Sponsor rotation with proof of play
  • Gate count, ticket sales and takings line up, by venue and by day
  • Reports for the Ministry and the auditors
  • Gates, tills and scoreboards keep working offline

AVA & Pal Monitor

  • AVA answers the public: fixtures, scores, parking
  • She checks every venue every two minutes and alerts the right person
  • Pal Monitor gives the Ministry every venue at a glance, counts only, no personal data
  • The morning-after report: attendance, income, sponsor plays, incidents and highlights
