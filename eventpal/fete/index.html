EVERYTHING IN EVENT PAL FOR FETES

The full list, from the drop to the morning after.

Set-up & the drop

  • One event brief, online or as a PDF sent to AVA on Telegram; AVA builds a draft for you to check
  • Promoters linked on Telegram: AVA knows who you are, shows only your own fetes, and asks before anything public or money-related
  • Listed on AVA with flyer, tiers and countdown; one-tap sharing to your socials

Tickets & gates

  • Early bird that moves to general by itself
  • General, VIP and all-inclusive, each with its own colour and gate lane, and its own wristband if wristbands are used
  • Outlets on a sales page or a sales device; a printed ticket only works once it's sold
  • Codes for street promoters; sales by outlet and promoter in real time, and cash owed
  • Lost tickets blocked in seconds; stolen, unsold tickets turned away
  • Secured codes that can't be copied or edited; screenshots fail; live capacity

Bars, food & stock

  • Every bar on Event Pal tills: yours and outside bars, bottle and cocktail bars, one floor plan
  • All-inclusive by wristband tap if wristbands are used, with limits per night or per hour
  • Bar sales in real time: top drink, busiest bar, busiest hour
  • Stock issued to each bar and confirmed by PIN; low-stock alerts to runners, who scan QR-tagged cases in at the bar
  • Food stalls on the same tills, with kitchen screens
  • Drink nudges to phones: opt-in, capped, and food and rides home late in the night
  • Cash control: floats, drops, blind close, no-sale drawer photos, outlets settled

Screens & the crowd

  • Big screens from the Event Pal Box, up to 4K, as NDI for your screen provider
  • DJ and lineup, shout-outs and a graphics pack made from your own flyers and promo media, so it matches your theme
  • Announcements on the big screen, from set times to last call
  • Sponsor rotation that keeps its schedule and works missed slots back in
  • Shake to win and crowd battles with AVA calling it; a phone light show on the drop
  • Crowd votes for the next tune, paid shout-outs, photos in a sponsor's frame
  • Stream passes for family abroad: one device per pass, no recording for viewers

AVA & Pal Monitor

  • AVA answers fans: times, outlets with tickets, what's in the all-inclusive
  • Locked down: no one sees your ticket sales or event information; a role for every login, every change logged, signed codes that can't be forged
  • A help button that sends a fan's spot to security; AVA points everyone home at closing
  • AVA is your personal assistant and your eyes at the event: she watches every gate, bar and screen, spots problems first, and answers your questions in seconds
  • Pal Monitor on your phone: gates, bars, stock, sales and alerts
  • AVA watches every part, messages you on Telegram, takes instructions, and contacts admin
  • Morning-after report in counts, with a crowd energy report hour by hour from shakes, votes and reactions; AVA keeps your crowd for next year's drop
