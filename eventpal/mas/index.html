EVERYTHING IN EVENT PAL FOR MAS BANDS

The full list, from registration to the last road.

Set-up

  • One band brief, online or as a PDF sent to AVA on Telegram; AVA builds a draft for you to check
  • Sections and costumes: frontline, midline, backline, prices, sizes and limits
  • Graphics built from your band launch flyers and media, so everything matches your theme

Registration & payments

  • Masqueraders register for a costume; early bird pricing if you want it
  • One QR code per masquerader, holding every costume they play, each with its own payment plan
  • Deposit, instalments and balance dates you set; Event Pal tracks what's owed
  • AVA reminds each masquerader a week before a payment is due
  • Online payments through your own card processor: the pay link carries their code, so payments land on the right person by themselves
  • The money goes straight to your account; Event Pal only keeps the record

Band house

  • Scan the code: paid, owed and every payment so far
  • Cash into the Event Pal drawer; card on your own card machine with the approval code recorded; receipt by WhatsApp
  • Costume pickup only when paid in full, in the right size; every pickup recorded, never collected twice

On the road

  • The rope: in the band, wrong section, or not in this band
  • All-inclusive drinks on the trucks, with your limits counted across every truck; water always free
  • Breakfast and lunch: one plate each, the meal they chose, portions counted as you serve
  • Restock: the support van is told, and cases are scanned onto the truck
  • Where's my band? AVA shows the truck on the map

AVA, screens & Pal Monitor

  • Your band and section on the big screens as you cross the stage
  • AVA is your eyes on the road: low stock, a stopped truck, a queue at the food
  • Pal Monitor: masqueraders on the road, drinks, meals and every truck
  • After the road: per section, money in, money owed, costumes collected, drinks and plates used
  • Private: no one else sees your registrations, payments or band information
