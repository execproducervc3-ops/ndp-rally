Ram Race

A national bar game where every player raises their own ram, keeps it home by buying Village Ram, and races it on bar TVs. The season ends in a national final at the bar that sells the most Village Ram, with a 2022 Suzuki Swift Sport as the grand prize.

How a player plays

Playing means buying: a ram is fed only when the bar completes a Village Ram sale on its till. Here is everything a player does, from tapping the ram on the bar wall to racing on the bar TV. No app to download, no sign-up forms.

The Tap N Ram plaque

A salesman on every bar wall. An NFC tag in the nose opens a Village Ram order. Every 30 minutes the bar is open, the ram baas and its eyes flash green until someone buys: up to 14 scheduled sales a night, about 44 a week per bar.

Linked to Restaurant Pal at the till, so every sale is counted and reported to ECGC.

Six months, three gates

Start small at one pilot bar, prove the sales lift, then go national. Each phase starts only when the one before it meets its gate.

Pilot · 6 weeksOne bar: the plaque and single-bar Ram Race; sales measured against before.
Onboarding · 2–4 weeksMy Ram goes live; bars set up in each zone.
National season · 12 weeksWeekly zone races, ending in the national final at the top-selling bar.
Review1–2 weeks. Results to ECGC; renewal decision.

Starting in February 2027 would put the national final in June, at the start of Carnival season. ECGC gets first refusal on a renewal.

Payment terms

Restaurant Pal's charges (everything except the grand prize and the other prizes associated with the game, which ECGC provides directly) are paid monthly over the 6-month term. The first payment is made up front, before work begins, and covers the EC$15,000 game licence plus the first month. ECGC / Village Ram bears all promotional costs.

Ownership and rights

The Tap N Ram plaques ECGC pays for belong to ECGC. The technology that runs Ram Race belongs to Restaurant Pal: the game and its software, the race system, the system that manages the plaques, and the plaque design. ECGC pays a tournament licence for each term.

Buyout: ECGC may buy the exclusive, permanent rights to the Ram Race name and game concept for EC$50,000, so no tournament licence is needed for future terms. Restaurant Pal keeps the technology and remains the sole technology provider; hosting, operation, plaques and onboarding are still charged.

Zone prizes are not included and will be agreed with ECGC. Renewal terms cost less: plaques and bar setup are one-time costs, and hosting would be reduced in later terms.

Our ask of ECGC

  1. Fund the programme: development, hosting and season operation, and Tap N Ram plaques for participating bars.
  2. Provide the prize: the 2022 Suzuki Swift Sport for the national champion, and smaller zone prizes. We can help arrange a car importer as co-sponsor.
  3. Approve brand use: the Village Ram name, logo and ram mascot in the game, on plaques and in race graphics.
  4. Co-host launch and final nights with product, setup and promotional material, and ECGC's Village Ram social media.
  5. Introduce Ram Race to bars through ECGC's distribution network, alongside Restaurant Pal's onboarding.
  6. Arrange a legal review of the prize promotion and official rules.
  7. Agree terms: season length, exclusivity in the energy drink category, and the reporting ECGC wants.
